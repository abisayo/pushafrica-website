Overview

Build things. Show your work. Get hired.

The School of Tech teaches digital skills the way the industry actually works — through projects. You'll write code, work with data and ship real work you can show to employers and clients.

Whether you want a role in a company, a remote position with a global team, or your own digital product, our training is built to make you genuinely employable.
