Overview

Africa's stories, told by Africa's voices

The School of Media trains storytellers, broadcasters and creators to professional standards — from camera and sound to editing, presenting and publishing.

You'll learn on real equipment and publish real work, so you graduate with a showreel and the confidence to work in newsrooms, studios, agencies or independently.
