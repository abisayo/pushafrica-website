Our Story

A different approach to development

PUSH Africa is a poverty-reduction organization based in Nigeria. We are committed to creating sustainable solutions for communities in need by combining business strategies with social missions.

Rather than temporary relief, we build capability: vocational skills, enterprise and agricultural development that let individuals and communities generate their own prosperity for the long term.

We are dedicated to making a lasting impact by pushing boundaries and redefining traditional aid models — from the classroom to the farm, and from the market to the halls of policy.

Our Core Activities

What we commit to doing

  • Promote pro-poor policies and initiatives.
  • Promote vocational education and skill-acquisition programmes in Africa.
  • Design and implement projects and capacity-building programmes for women and youth.
  • Foster intra-Africa and bilateral trade.
  • Promote inventions and development from and by Africa.
  • Promote peaceful co-existence among African peoples.
  • Deliver charitable interventions that alleviate poverty for women, youth and children.
  • Promote African tourism to the rest of the world.
