Thematic Areas

Four areas of focus

Every programme we run sits within one of these four pillars — and together they form a complete pathway out of poverty.

01

Education

Promoting T-VET (technical and vocational education and training) and skill-acquisition programmes across Africa — equipping people with practical, employable skills.

  • Four specialist schools under one institute
  • Industry-relevant, hands-on curriculum
  • International certification pathways
Explore our schools
02

Entrepreneurship

Fostering intra-Africa and bilateral trade, incubating small businesses and supporting the entrepreneurs who create jobs in their communities.

  • Business training and mentorship
  • Seed support for early-stage ventures
  • Market and trade linkages
See 10K Startup
03

Governance

Promoting pro-poor policies and initiatives, and advocating for systems that prioritise the dignity and potential of every citizen.

  • Civic and policy engagement
  • Community advocacy
  • Peaceful co-existence initiatives
See our impact
04

Agriculture

Agricultural development in rural communities — supporting peasant and cluster farmers to improve yields, income and food security.

  • Farmer training and extension
  • Cluster and cooperative support
  • Market access and value addition
See agriculture programmes
