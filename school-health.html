Overview

Care as a profession, not just a calling

The School of Health prepares learners for meaningful, skilled roles in healthcare — from patient care and nursing support to public health practice.

Training blends clinical knowledge with supervised practical experience and a strong grounding in ethics, safety and communication. Our focus is employability and mobility: qualifications and competence that travel.
