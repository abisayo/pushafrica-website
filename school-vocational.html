Overview

Skills you can use on day one

The Vocational Institute is where practical ability meets opportunity. We train learners in trade, craft and technical disciplines with a strong emphasis on doing — not just theory.

Our workshops, tools and instructors are drawn from industry, so what you learn in class is what you'll be asked to do on site. Graduates leave with a portfolio of real work and recognised certification.
